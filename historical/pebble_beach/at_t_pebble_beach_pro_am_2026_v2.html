The AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am sets up as a premium ball-striker's tournament where approach play and scrambling around poa annua greens will separate contenders from pretenders. With bombers holding minimal advantage on the 6,972-yard layout, precise iron play becomes paramount to reaching the typical -17 winning score. Scottie Scheffler's +375 odds appear justified given his elite approach play metrics, while the three-course rotation and potential weather disruptions create opportunities for savvy scramblers to outperform their betting lines.
